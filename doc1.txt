The solar system consists of the Sun and the objects that orbit it, including eight planets, their moons, dwarf planets, asteroids, and comets. Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and has extreme temperature swings between day and night. Venus is the hottest planet due to its thick atmosphere trapping heat through a runaway greenhouse effect. Earth is the only known planet to support life, with liquid water covering most of its surface. Mars, often called the Red Planet, has the largest volcano in the solar system, Olympus Mons.

Jupiter is the largest planet and has a giant storm called the Great Red Spot that has raged for centuries. Saturn is famous for its prominent ring system made of ice and rock particles. Uranus rotates on its side, likely due to a massive collision early in its history. Neptune is the farthest known planet and has the strongest winds in the solar system, reaching over 1,200 miles per hour.
