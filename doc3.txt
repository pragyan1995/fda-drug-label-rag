The Roman Empire was one of the largest empires in world history, spanning from Britain to the Middle East at its peak. It was founded after the fall of the Roman Republic in 27 BC when Augustus became the first emperor. The empire is credited with major advances in engineering, including roads, aqueducts, and concrete construction still studied today. The Western Roman Empire fell in 476 AD, while the Eastern Roman Empire, known as the Byzantine Empire, continued for nearly a thousand more years until 1453.
